ilune GitHub

Security posture management for non-human identities and AI agents

Ilune keeps an inventory of the service principals, tokens, keys and secrets across your identity sources, then checks it for credentials that are expired, have no owner or are no longer used.

Coming soon Follow Ilune on GitHub
Inventory
One record for every non-human identity, with its owners, credentials and permissions. Ilune starts with Microsoft Entra ID and Azure.
Read-only
Ilune only reads. It never creates, changes, rotates or deletes anything in the systems it scans, and it never stores secret values.
Open source
Free to use and self-hosted. Scans run in your own environment, so your cloud credentials stay with you.
